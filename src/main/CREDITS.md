## Firearms
The gun models, textures and sounds (revolver, pistols, shotguns, rifles, SMG) and the ammo item textures
come from **Just Enough Guns** by **MigaMi**, used with the author's permission:
- Just Enough Guns: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/just-enough-guns
- Just Enough Guns is a fork of **MrCrayfish's Gun Mod** by MrCrayfish.
- The Fabric 1.21.1 files were taken from the unofficial port "Just Enough Guns New" by Rui Mao.

The shooting, reloading and ammo code in this mod is written independently; the gun design (survival crafting,
ammo and reload) is inspired by Just Enough Guns.
